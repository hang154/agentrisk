# Organization lineage correction — analysis integrity amendment

A source-code review during execution found that `bank()` varies invoice names, amounts and payees across six family labels, but uses the **same payment mechanism and oracle**. The original preparation description of six mechanisms was incorrect. All 1,728 preregistered executions, seeds, policies and raw records remain unchanged; no outcomes are selected or discarded.

Primary inferential clustering therefore uses **one shared-payment mechanism**. With one mechanism, a cross-workflow superiority claim is not identifiable. Six-contract cluster intervals remain explicitly descriptive sensitivity calculations, not independent six-workflow confirmation. Fixed-size organizations are prescribed experimental configurations, not evidence of autonomous spawning. This correction does not replace the original 40-task V1 population or its separately reported held-out workflow results.

The lineage discovery is an audit finding, not a weakened success criterion. Report effect sizes and failure paths, and retain `cross_workflow_superiority = NOT_ESTABLISHED` regardless of favorable variant-level results.
